Methodology. Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × tenure factor. The peer multiple is the category median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of token-bearing protocols in the same category (global median when a category has fewer than two listed peers), clamped to 0.02–2.0. The growth factor adjusts for the protocol’s 7-day TVL change; the tenure factor grows with years since the protocol was listed. Only protocols that record no token are ranked.
